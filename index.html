Using the timeline

The chapter filter follows the cited passage. Page numbers refer to the class’s 60-page PDF.

Foreshadowing means an earlier detail hints at a later event. Dramatic irony occurs when readers know something that a character does not. The honour code is the set of expectations in this town that connects family reputation with women’s sexual behaviour and men’s duty to defend that reputation.
